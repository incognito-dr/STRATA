Our Story

Adventure dynamics.
Dynamic adventures.

Bonnell wasn't born in a boardroom. It was forged by riders who couldn't leave stock bikes alone. We spent years chasing a specific ride feel: the perfect balance between rider and machine. When we couldn't find it, we built it ourselves.

That obsession became a relentless pursuit of engineering electric motorcycles that inspire confidence, reward exploration, and redefine what's possible off-road.

Our Mission

We exist to equip, inspire, and empower a new generation of riders to charge farther, explore deeper, and leave the noise of the world behind.

Our Values

Think globally. Innovate every day. Maximize value. Act holistically. Revel in the pursuit.

These aren't just words. They're how we build our motorcycles and how we work together. Every decision is driven by a commitment to thoughtful engineering, continuous improvement, and creating products that riders can trust wherever adventure takes them.
